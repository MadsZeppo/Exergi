# Opportunity Engine V1

The engine is deterministic/statistical; no LLM generates opportunities or numeric claims.

The V1 registry is designed for repeat-rate deterioration, purchase-cadence slowdown, high-intent/low-conversion, funnel deterioration, category migration, cohort-quality deterioration, refund leakage, discount dependency, randomized promotion-response differences and margin opportunities.

The executable vertical slice detects persistent repeat deterioration in an economically meaningful high-intent population. It reports population size, current and baseline metrics, absolute/relative difference, interval, persistence and addressable value.

`ADDRESSABLE VALUE` and `OBSERVED GAP` are never renamed incremental uplift. Candidate actions are curated and default to `TEST THIS` without merchant/action-specific randomized evidence.
