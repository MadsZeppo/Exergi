# Exergi V14 preregistration

Status: `PREREGISTERED_BEFORE_ANY_SYNTHETIC_OUTCOME_GENERATION`

Calibration authority is frozen at commit `9485e548491c0765f6a114014a35ae02fa19d589`. V14 is a
reality-calibrated semi-synthetic mechanism benchmark, not randomized merchant evidence.

## Question

Can the complete observable Exergi pipeline move from state and BAU forecasting through economic
multi-action choice, bounded experimentation, mature feedback and verified memory while producing more
incremental contribution profit than BAU and best static—without unsupported ACT, future leakage or
premature risk release?

## Splits and outcomes

- DEVELOPMENT: eight merchants, two per family.
- VALIDATION: four merchant-disjoint edge-region merchants.
- SEALED_TEST: four merchant-disjoint stress-region merchants.
- Every merchant has 20,000 eligible synthetic customers and 52 weeks.
- New synthetic IDs are created independently; source entities are never joined.
- At this checkpoint, no synthetic outcomes for any split exist.

Only DEVELOPMENT may generate outcomes in the next phase. If no candidate passes every promotion gate,
V14 stops and VALIDATION remains ungenerated and unopened. One-shot VALIDATION and SEALED rules apply only
after separately committed freeze and outcome-free dry-run checkpoints.

## Model and policy hierarchy

The frozen hierarchy is BAU → best static → rules/segments → personalized multi-channel policy. Candidate
families include regularized linear, tree/forest T-learners, X-, R- and DR-learners, a causal-forest
equivalent and a conservative ensemble. A complex policy can win only on honest held-out incremental
contribution profit net of all mature costs and uncertainty. The simpler policy wins practical ties.

Oracle truth is forbidden for training, model selection, thresholds, support, dispositions and risk.
Oracle capture is evaluator-only after a policy is frozen.

## Promotion and safety

The full numeric contract lives in `configs/V14_PASS_GATES.json`. Personalization requires positive value
and a positive 95% lower bound versus BAU and best static, materiality, 4/5 positive folds, at least 80%
seed and world-family direction agreement, passing placebos, zero unsupported DO, bounded null/harmful DO
rates, complete costs and no risk violations.

Risk is reserved before exposure and released only after mature economic outcomes. Credible harm stops
the next decision batch. Reactivation requires new positive mature evidence and support.

No threshold is imported from Hillstrom, V8, V9, JTPA or an earlier failure. All constants here are frozen
ex ante as design choices and must be reported whether favorable or not.
