What Exergi knew before reveal

DEVELOPMENT supported a +$0.688950 net point estimate. The policy, estimand, two-sided 95% gate, cost, features, secondary analyses, seeds, hashes and claim boundary were committed before VALIDATION outcomes were read.

What happened afterwards

The one-shot validation produced +$0.719312 per customer. Lin ANCOVA (+$0.733109), cross-fitted AIPW (+$0.734735), bootstrap CI [$0.234264, $1.218767], and assignment randomization p=0.00554972 corroborated the frozen primary result.

Evidence authority

REAL_RANDOMIZED_NET_REVENUE_AFTER_DECLARED_EMAIL_COST

On a previously unseen validation sample from a real randomized e-commerce experiment, Exergi's development-selected static action produced statistically supported incremental net revenue over business-as-usual after a preregistered email cost.

Limitations

Hillstrom contains spend/revenue, not contribution profit. It lacks observed COGS, shipping, returns, payment fees and other variable costs. This proof does not establish personalization, merchant generalization, autonomous decision safety or production readiness. Validation is permanently consumed. SEALED_TEST was not opened by V8 and remains historically quarantined.
