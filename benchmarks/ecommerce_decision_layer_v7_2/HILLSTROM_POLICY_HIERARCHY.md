# Hillstrom DEVELOPMENT Policy Hierarchy

The fixed promotion order is:

`BAU → best supported static action → supported segment policy → supported personalized policy`.

A complex level cannot bypass a failed simpler level. Promotion requires positive held-out
incremental net value over the preceding level, a positive lower 95% bound, four of five positive
folds, no fold below `-$0.05`, and randomized support.

| Level | Candidate | Development evidence | Promoted |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAU | No E-Mail | Baseline | Yes |
| Static | Mens E-Mail | Full-development mean evidence positive; freeze stability fails | No |
| Segment | Simple RFM/affinity | -$0.256668 vs static, CI [-$0.982996, $0.469659]; 3/5 positive folds | No |
| Personalized | Tweedie T-learner | -$0.011967 vs static, CI [-$0.492335, $0.468401]; one fold -$1.362198 | No |

The selected hierarchy level is **BAU**. This does not claim Mens E-Mail has zero effect. It means
the static candidate failed its fixed stability contract, and neither segmentation nor
personalization demonstrates incremental economic value over static. VALIDATION remains closed.
