# V10 MT-LIFT stop report

## Decision

V10 stopped in Phase 1, before any click or conversion outcome analysis.

`V10_DATASET_NOT_CAUSALLY_QUALIFIED`

## Why

- The official Google Drive payload is not publicly retrievable and redirects to sign-in.
- The official Baidu link is expired.
- A persistent randomization unit and repeat-user structure are not documented.
- `f0`–`f98` lack feature-level timing evidence and are all forbidden.
- Raw integrity and the unresolved treatment-feature imbalance cannot be audited.

## Actions deliberately not taken

- no DEVELOPMENT materialization or outcome read;
- no TEST outcome read;
- no preregistration after a failed qualification;
- no model tournament or policy-value estimation;
- no model selection or freeze;
- no one-shot reveal or consumed lock;
- no cost sensitivity, claim card, public case study, or monetary claim.

## Interpretation

This is neither a personalization FAIL nor a static-policy result. The evidence needed to run a
causally authoritative V10 was not available. Reclassifying it as PASS, FAIL, static value, or
confirmed avoid would manufacture a result that was never estimated.
