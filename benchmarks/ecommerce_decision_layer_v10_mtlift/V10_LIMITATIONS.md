# V10 MT-LIFT limitations

1. The publisher-linked data payload was unavailable without Google account access, while the
   publisher's Baidu link was expired.
2. The released documents do not identify a persistent randomization unit or clustering key.
3. Repeat observations of a person cannot be established or excluded.
4. Coupon arms are anonymous and assignment probabilities are undocumented.
5. The 99 features lack field meanings and timestamps; all are forbidden in this checkpoint.
6. A public feature-imbalance report remains unresolved and could not be audited without data.
7. No raw checksum, schema, row count, treatment count, missingness, duplicate, overlap, SRM,
   or outcome-chain check was possible.
8. No model or policy was fit. This checkpoint says nothing about whether MT-LIFT contains
   exploitable heterogeneity.
9. Even a future PASS could establish conversion policy value only, not revenue, coupon cost,
   contribution profit, or autonomous action safety.

The failure is a qualification/access result, not evidence that personalization is ineffective.
