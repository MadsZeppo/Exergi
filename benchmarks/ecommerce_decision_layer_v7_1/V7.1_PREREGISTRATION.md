# Exergi V7.1 preregistration — economically identifiable personalization

Frozen before generation or execution of O/P/Q and R/S/T. V7 commit
`aabda4537542c6aebcb3269c96e1b4e684ed5e59` is immutable. H-M are consumed diagnostic-only packs.
Pack N is retired forever and is not a V7.1 input. Pack U is not generated by development or
validation commands.

## Primary estimand and taxonomy

The primary personalization estimand is held-out **incremental net contribution profit over the
best supported static policy**, including treatment and switching cost.

Economic materiality is frozen at **0.10 net CP per eligible customer**. This is V7's existing
minimum economically relevant effect and is not selected from H-M classifications.

Evaluator-only references:

- `FULL_ORACLE`: optimal treatment from true individual net effects.
- `OBSERVABLE_ORACLE`: flexible evaluator trained on a large independent truth-known sample using
  only legitimate pre-treatment observables. It shares no fitted state with the policy.
- `SEGMENT_ORACLE`: best policy among the fixed RFM, intent, loyalty and category segment set.

Worlds are classified after policy predictions freeze:

- `MATERIAL_OBSERVABLE_PERSONALIZATION`: observable-oracle increment >0.10.
- `NONMATERIAL_PERSONALIZATION`: full and observable increments ≤0.10.
- `MATERIAL_UNOBSERVABLE_PERSONALIZATION`: full increment >0.10 but observable increment ≤0.10.
- `UNSUPPORTED_PERSONALIZATION`: the required assignment, overlap, attrition or cost support fails.

`ESTIMATION_OR_POLICY_FAILURE` is a separate deployable-performance result, not an oracle class:
material observable increment exists but the learned policy does not produce positive held-out
increment.

## Development tournament

O/P/Q are development-only. Candidates:

- Ridge T-learner;
- forest T-learner;
- X-learner;
- R-learner;
- DR-learner;
- reproducible causal forest if the optional dependency is installed;
- honest depth-2 policy tree;
- best predefined segment policy.

The selector first excludes candidates with any unsupported ACT, computational failure, null/harm
personalization above 5%, or nonmaterial personalization above 10%. Among eligible candidates it
maximizes, in order: mean held-out incremental CP in material-observable worlds, lower confidence
bound, correct static fallback, calibration, then lower runtime. AUUC alone cannot select a model.
The selector and winner freeze before R/S/T.

## Validation gates

### Material observable personalization

- positive held-out incremental CP over best static in at least 75% of worlds;
- mean one-sided lower confidence bound across worlds >0;
- mean capture of observable-oracle increment ≥50%;
- positive sign in at least 75% of pack/fold estimates;
- ESS ≥200 and propensity in [0.05, 0.95] for every promoted policy.

### Nonmaterial personalization

- best static or BAU selected;
- false personalization promotion ≤5% (family-wise tolerance).

### Material unobservable personalization

- personalization rejection ≥95%;
- only best static, BAU or bounded TEST is permitted;
- no individualized-value claim.

### Unsupported personalization

- personalization rejection 100%;
- unsupported ACT exactly zero;
- only BAU, AVOID or bounded TEST is permitted.

### Homogeneous positive

- best static/treat-all selected in ≥90%;
- false personalization promotion ≤5%.

### Null and harmful

- BAU/AVOID;
- unsupported ACT exactly zero;
- false promotion ≤5%.

## Sequential assurance gates

- merchant and family committed-risk budgets hold on every preregistered path;
- no new exposure above available reserved risk;
- avoidable post-observable loss ≤one operational assignment batch;
- stop latency ≤one period after a legitimate mature harm signal;
- p95, p99, CVaR, maximum drawdown, pre-observable loss, risk utilization, retained value,
  reactivation and false suspension are all reported with Monte Carlo intervals;
- no mean-only safety PASS.

Sequential seeds are 171001-171400, 400 paths per required stress family, merchant budget 120 CP,
family budget 60 CP, batch size 10, maximum downside reservation 2 CP/customer, and maturity delay
1-4 periods by world. These values encode a maximum of three concurrently open 20-CP batches per
family and six per merchant.

Required stress families are positive, null, harmful, insufficient support, action cost, switching
cost, maturity delay, concurrent immature batches, abrupt reversal, gradual decay, common shock,
causal shift, missing returns, attrition, noncompliance, propensity corruption, action fatigue,
reactivation, drift before maturity and drift after maturity. Scenario truth generates outcomes but
is unavailable to `observable_lifecycle_decision`.

## Real-data boundaries

Hillstrom is randomized revenue evidence with profit scenarios only. Criteo uses `treatment` as ITT
and never `exposure`; it has proxy-outcome authority. Open Bandit has click/OPE authority. X5 remains
`UNKNOWN_ASSIGNMENT` and cannot produce causal evidence. Real datasets do not tune synthetic gates.
