# V12 qualification limitations

1. The public records file contains no persistent claimant identifier.
2. Duplicate claimants and multiple assignments cannot be audited.
3. The documented internal SSN-based assignment key is not released.
4. Treatment allocation changed over time, while exact per-claimant propensities are absent.
5. The survey ID cannot join to the records file, and survey selection oversampled bonus recipients.
6. Total UI benefits actually paid are not exposed as one explicitly documented records field.
7. Administrative cost exists only as report-level estimates, not observed unit-level data.
8. Upjohn's standalone final-report URL serves the wrong state's report; the correct Pennsylvania
   report was available inside the verified ZIP.
9. No outcome values, models, policies, effects, confidence intervals, or DKK values were produced.

These limitations prevent this public release from answering V12's personalized economic policy
question. They do not invalidate the original experiment or its published aggregate findings.
