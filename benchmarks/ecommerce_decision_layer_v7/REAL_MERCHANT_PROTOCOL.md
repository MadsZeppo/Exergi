# Exergi V7 first real-merchant protocol

Status: contract and shadow mode only. No autonomous launch or assignment is authorized.

## Pilot

- One replicated action family: win-back communication/offer.
- Fixed randomized control; optional message-only and low-discount arms.
- Control never withholds necessary service.
- Primary estimand: intention-to-treat.
- Primary outcome: mature contribution profit per eligible customer.
- Secondary outcomes: conversion and revenue.
- CUPED with pre-period contribution profit.
- Sample size is based on a merchant-declared minimum economically relevant effect.
- Permanent sentinel/control; no individualized adaptive allocation.
- Shadow mode must complete and a merchant must manually approve launch and scale.

## Required row contract

Each outcome carries gross pre-discount item revenue, item COGS, merchant-funded discount, shipping
subsidy, mature returns/refunds, transaction fees, channel cost, assignment and eligibility
timestamps, arm, logged propensity, delivery/exposure/compliance, experiment/customer/interference
IDs and outcome maturity timestamp. Missing costs or immature returns yield no contribution-profit
estimate.

## Commercial evidence gate

A real merchant-profit claim requires at least three preregistered experiments across at least two
merchants, a replicated action family, a positive pooled/hierarchical contribution-profit lower
bound, all negative trials included, no hard-budget breach and no merchant with a serious loss.
Pooled positivity cannot override serious merchant harm.

Only then may claim authority become `REAL_RANDOMIZED_CONTRIBUTION_PROFIT`. Current authority is
synthetic or insufficient; Exergi has not documented real merchant profit.
