# Exergi Validation Report

## Current answer

If a real ecommerce company supplied data tomorrow, there is development-only
synthetic evidence that Exergi can produce positive contribution profit, keep
unsupported ACT at zero, preserve randomized BAU comparison traffic, and stop
aggregate harm once an observed causal harmsignal becomes available.

That is **not enough** to conclude Exergi is more likely to help than hurt a real
company.  Fresh synthetic packs produced positive value but too little action and
value capture, p99 drawdown exceeded the risk limit, and reactivation could not be
established.  No official V6.2 final has been revealed and no independent real
dataset has been opened in this pass.

## Evidence status

- Known-truth synthetic economic value: supported on Pack A and directionally
  positive on E/F/G.
- Abrupt-change containment: post-observable aggregate policy loss was zero in
  Pack A and 1,368 paired torture cells.
- Fundamental unobservable harm: p99 943.90 CP and maximum 1,887.81 CP in the
  torture grid before an informative outcome existed.
- Risk limiting: zero accounting violations, but maximum torture loss and E/F/G
  p99 pathwise drawdown show the credible bound is not a hard worst-case bound.
- Common shocks: separated from incremental treatment shifts, but Pack A still
  had eight non-suspension downgrades; this remains imperfect.
- Recovery: 5/5 on Pack A; not evaluable on E/F/G.
- Discovery: Top-3 and economic-weighted precision are stable; rank #1 remains
  weak and cross-pack testing is too conservative.
- Calibration: sequential null calibration is established by the existing 6,000
  trial validation.  Cross-pack policy reliability is not yet established.
- Real-world support: none added. X5, Hillstrom, and Open Bandit remain sealed
  because the synthetic freeze gates failed.
